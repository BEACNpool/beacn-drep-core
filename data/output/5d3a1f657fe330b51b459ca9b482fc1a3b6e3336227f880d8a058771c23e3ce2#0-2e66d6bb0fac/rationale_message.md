# Reforming Treasury Governance
**BEACN DRep vote: ABSTAIN**  _(plain-language explanation — precomputed)_

BEACN voted ABSTAIN because the proposal raises a reasonable governance discussion, but the evidence threshold for a directional vote was not met. This is a conservative, evidence-based hold, not active opposition.

The action was an InfoAction asking to introduce basic ideas for reforming Cardano treasury governance, encourage public discussion, and invite collaboration on possible process or constitutional changes. The proposal argues for clearer treasury direction, a coherent strategy, a roadmap, dedicated investment budgets, thematic domains, fixed-term strategic bodies, and a sequence of votes on the NCL, budget, and treasury withdrawals. Those process ideas were sufficiently present in the proposal itself, and the pinned anchor document was available and replayable.

The weaker part was the evidence behind some of the proposal’s diagnosis. The claims that treasury governance is in a poor state because of a funding impasse, and that treasury withdrawals have created harmful competition and community conflict, were treated as proposer assertions without independent supporting evidence. The review also found execution risk unknown, mitigation evidence missing, independent assurance missing, and the claims-and-evidence and risk-review sections thin. A flag score was present, which reduced confidence.

A directional vote would be unblocked by replayable independent evidence for the major diagnostic claims, plus clearer mitigation evidence and independent assurance around the governance and execution risks. Until then, abstaining avoids overstating certainty while still recognizing that the proposal contains discussion-worthy treasury governance ideas.
