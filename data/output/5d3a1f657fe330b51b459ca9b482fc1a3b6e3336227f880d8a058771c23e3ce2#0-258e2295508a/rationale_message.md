# Reforming Treasury Governance
**BEACN DRep vote: ABSTAIN**  _(plain-language explanation — precomputed)_

BEACN voted ABSTAIN because the review did not have enough distribution and independent evidence to support a directional vote. This is a conservative, evidence-based hold, not a rejection of treasury reform as a topic.

The action asks to introduce ideas for reforming Cardano treasury governance into public discussion, including process changes, budget structure, strategic guardrails, and possible constitutional amendments. The proposal claims that treasury governance is in a poor state, that recent withdrawals have increased community conflict, that DReps should vote on balanced budgets instead of isolated withdrawals, and that voted fixed-term bodies should help prepare budgets and evaluate projects.

One claim was well-supported inside the proposal: the suggested budget structure includes eight domains, including core protocol and infrastructure, developer tooling, research and innovation, commercial ecosystem growth, operations, education and marketing, other public-good initiatives, and a reserve. But several important claims remained unsupported by independent replayable evidence, especially the claim that treasury governance is currently in a poor state with a funding impasse, and the claim that recent withdrawal actions have caused hostility and strife.

The proposal had a pinned, replayable anchor and passed the counterargument step, but the evidence review and risk review were thin. Execution risk was unknown, mitigation evidence and independent assurance were missing, and no DRep distribution was available. A directional vote would be unblocked by public, replayable evidence for the major governance claims, clearer risk mitigation evidence, independent assurance, and the missing DRep distribution data.
