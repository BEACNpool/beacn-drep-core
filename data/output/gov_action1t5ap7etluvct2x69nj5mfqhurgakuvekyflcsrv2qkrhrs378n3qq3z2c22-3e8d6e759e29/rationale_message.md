# Reforming Treasury Governance
**BEACN DRep vote: ABSTAIN**  _(plain-language explanation — precomputed)_

BEACN voted ABSTAIN because the proposal offered a useful discussion structure for treasury governance reform, but the evidence threshold for a directional vote was not met. This is a conservative, evidence-based hold, not opposition to the topic.

The action asked only for an InfoAction: to introduce ideas for reforming Cardano treasury governance, outline guardrails, suggest votes on the NCL, budget, and treasury withdrawals, propose eight thematic budget domains, and invite further collaboration. The proposal was reviewable because its pinned anchor document was available and replayable, and several governance-design claims were supported in the proposal itself, including that treasury governance should be explicitly addressed by the governance system and constitution, that thematic budgets could be preferable to isolated project withdrawals, and that voted entities or commissions could help prepare budgets and evaluate projects.

The main weakness was evidence quality. Some of the proposal’s most consequential claims were only proposer assertions, especially that Cardano treasury governance is currently in a poor state due to a funding impasse and insufficient governance-system support, and that current treasury withdrawal voting has caused competition, hostility, toxicity, ill will, and strife. The review also found the claims-and-evidence status thin, noted missing mitigation evidence and independent assurance in the risk review, and recorded a flag score that reduced confidence.

A directional YES would be unblocked by replayable independent evidence for the strongest diagnostic claims about the current treasury situation and community harms, plus clearer mitigation evidence or independent assurance for the governance process being proposed. Until then, abstaining avoids treating serious claims as established facts while still recognizing that the proposal raises a legitimate topic for public discussion.
