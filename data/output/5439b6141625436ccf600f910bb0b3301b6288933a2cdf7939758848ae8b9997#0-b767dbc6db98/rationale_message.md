# Blockfrost's transformation to not-for-profit
**BEACN DRep vote: NEEDS_MORE_INFO**  _(plain-language explanation — precomputed)_

BEACN voted NEEDS_MORE_INFO because this is a large treasury withdrawal and the required Net Change Limit was not pinned and independently verified from public chain evidence. That blocks a directional treasury vote even though the proposal has plausible public-benefit value.

The action asks for 9,832,979 ADA to fund an 18-month transition of Blockfrost into a free, community-governed not-for-profit public API for Cardano. The proposal describes Blockfrost as hosted infrastructure that lets developers use Cardano without running their own node, and that core technical claim was supported well enough. It also commits to a not-for-profit entity, asset transfer, a community-approved board, public transition milestones, quarterly reporting, and minimum 99% monthly uptime during 2027.

The review found several important claims were still not independently supported. The proposal’s figures for recent usage, including 781,000 unique visitors, more than 1.84 billion API requests, almost 700 requests per second, over 7 TB of API data, and more than 50% of Cardano transactions in most epochs, remained proposer-asserted. The review also found thin evidence overall, missing sustainability-path and cost-benefit clarity, and missing independent assurance plus a rollback or remedy path. Execution risk was rated medium, and the treasury-flow signal was in a stressed regime.

This is a conservative, evidence-based hold, not a rejection of the idea. A directional vote would be unblocked by a pinned and independently verified Net Change Limit from public chain evidence, plus stronger replayable evidence for the material usage and dependency claims, clearer long-term sustainability and cost-benefit analysis, and independent assurance on execution risk and remedies if the transition does not go as planned.
