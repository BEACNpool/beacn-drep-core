BEACN voted NO because this was a large treasury withdrawal, and the evidence did not meet the higher bar BEACN applies when shared ADA is being spent. The action requested 540,750 ada for Year 2 maintenance and enhancement of UTxO RPC by TxPipe: 420,000 ada for work, a 105,000 ada contingency reserve, and a 15,750 ada Intersect administration fee. The proposal says this would fund 12 months of part-time maintainer coverage, protobuf specification maintenance, SDK maintenance, community support, documentation and integration resources, and milestone-based disbursement through Intersect oversight.

BEACN found some claims well-supported: the funding breakdown, the 12-month maintainer scope, the technical description of UTxO RPC as a Protocol Buffers interface with multiple SDKs, the Intersect budget-process support threshold, and the stated treasury management process with smart contracts, oversight, reporting, and public tracking. However, material claims about adoption by Amaru, Dingo, and the Haskell Cardano node were only proposer-asserted, as was the claim about maintaining more than 10 open-source repositories. The review also found thin cost-benefit clarity, no stated sustainability path, unknown execution risk, no independent assurance, and no clear rollback or remedy path.

The strongest YES case was that the proposal clearly described the requested amount and governance controls, and it passed several evidence gates. BEACN still voted NO because treasury withdrawals require elevated scrutiny, current treasury-flow conditions added caution, and unsupported material adoption claims made the cost and precedent harder to justify.
