# Reforming Treasury Governance
**BEACN DRep vote: ABSTAIN**  _(plain-language explanation — precomputed)_

BEACN voted ABSTAIN because the proposal did not meet the evidence threshold for a directional vote. The action raises important treasury governance questions, but several core problem statements were asserted rather than independently supported, so BEACN treated this as a conservative, evidence-based hold.

The action requested an InfoAction to sketch ideas for reforming Cardano treasury governance and invite public discussion. It proposed basic propositions, a voting sequence for treasury governance, thematic budget categories, and wider ecosystem collaboration. The proposal’s better-supported points were that treasury governance is important for Cardano’s growth and stability, that DReps should consider balanced budgets rather than isolated withdrawals, and that a possible sequence could move from the NCL to budgets and then to treasury withdrawals.

The weaker side was evidence. The proposal claimed that treasury governance is currently in a poor state, that there is a funding impasse, and that current treasury withdrawal voting has created competition, hostility, toxicity, and gridlock. Those claims were not backed by independent replayable evidence in the review. The review also found thin claims-and-evidence status, missing mitigation evidence, and missing independent assurance, even though execution risk was assessed as low and the anchor document was available.

This abstention is not active opposition. The review found a credible YES case around improving treasury strategy and governance clarity, but the rule threshold was not met. A directional vote would be unblocked by independent evidence for the major problem claims, clearer mitigation evidence, and stronger assurance around the implications of appointed entities and possible constitutional support.
