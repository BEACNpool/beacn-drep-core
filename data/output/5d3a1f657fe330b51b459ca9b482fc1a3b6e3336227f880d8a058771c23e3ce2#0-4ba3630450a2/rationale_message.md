# Reforming Treasury Governance
**BEACN DRep vote: ABSTAIN**  _(plain-language explanation — precomputed)_

BEACN voted ABSTAIN because the review did not have enough evidence to support a directional vote, especially with no DRep distribution available. This is a conservative, evidence-based hold, not a rejection of treasury governance reform.

The action asked for public discussion of ideas to reform Cardano treasury governance, including strategic direction, budget structure, process guardrails, and a proposed flow involving an NCL vote, budget vote, and treasury withdrawal vote. The proposal claimed that current treasury governance is in a poor state, with funding impasse, perceived competition, hostility, toxicity, and gridlock. Those severe dysfunction claims were treated as proposer assertions and lacked independent supporting evidence in the review.

The strongest supported part of the proposal was its eight-part thematic budget structure, covering core infrastructure, developer tooling, research, commercial growth, administration, community and marketing, other strategic initiatives, and a functional reserve. The anchor document was available, pinned, and replayable, so the action could be reviewed. But the claims-and-evidence gate remained thin, execution risk was unknown, mitigation evidence and independent assurance were missing, and a flag score reduced confidence.

A directional vote would be unblocked by DRep distribution data, independent evidence for the major claims about treasury dysfunction and gridlock, and clearer mitigation or assurance evidence for governance and execution risks. Without that, abstaining avoids overstating certainty while leaving room for the discussion to improve with stronger public evidence.
