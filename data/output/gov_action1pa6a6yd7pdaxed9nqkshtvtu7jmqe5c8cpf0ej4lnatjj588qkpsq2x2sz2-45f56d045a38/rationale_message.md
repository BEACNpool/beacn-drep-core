# Alchemy by Sundial x Charms: Cardano-Native Bitcoin Treasury Protocol
**BEACN DRep vote: NO**  _(plain-language explanation — precomputed)_

BEACN voted NO because this was a large 10,000,000 ada treasury request, and the evidence did not meet the higher bar required for spending shared treasury funds.

The proposal asked the treasury to fund Alchemy, a Cardano-native Bitcoin treasury protocol and BTCfi infrastructure layer by Sundial Protocol and Charms. It described two roughly USD 1 million planning pools: one for protocol infrastructure and staged launch liquidity, and one for delivery, audits, integrations, dashboards, reporting, legal and compliance work, and launch execution. The review found several parts well-supported in the proposal itself, including the budget structure, the FIRE and ICE reserve design, the 5.0x initial reserve-ratio target, staged deployment, reporting commitments, and stated risk mitigations.

The concern was not that the proposal lacked detail. It was comparatively detailed. The concern was that important claims and assumptions remained thin or unsupported by independent evidence, especially the claim that Cardano lacks credible production BTCfi infrastructure and risks losing this activity to other ecosystems. The review also flagged missing treasury analysis around sustainability path and cost-benefit clarity, plus missing risk review evidence around independent assurance and a dependency map. Execution risk was marked unknown, and treasury conditions were already in an unsustainable signal regime, which added caution.

BEACN did consider the strongest YES case: the requested amount and budget split were clearly stated and supported in the proposal. But treasury withdrawals require elevated scrutiny because they spend shared ada and set precedent. With a large ask, novel reserve mechanics, bridge and oracle dependencies, adoption assumptions, administrator assumptions, and treasury-return assumptions not fully evidenced inside the review record, the evidence supported a conservative NO.
