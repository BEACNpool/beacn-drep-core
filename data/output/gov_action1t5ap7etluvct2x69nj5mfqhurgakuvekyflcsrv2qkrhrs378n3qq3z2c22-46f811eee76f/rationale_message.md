# Reforming Treasury Governance
**BEACN DRep vote: ABSTAIN**  _(plain-language explanation — precomputed)_

BEACN voted ABSTAIN because the proposal raises a credible governance discussion, but the evidence threshold for a directional vote was not met. This is a conservative, evidence-based hold, not active opposition.

The action asked for an InfoAction to sketch ideas for reforming Cardano treasury governance and invite public discussion. It proposed basic reform principles, a treasury voting sequence, thematic budget domains, and possible roles for a strategic entity and expert commission. The anchor document was available and reviewable, and the execution risk was assessed as low.

Several core ideas were supported within the proposal: that treasury governance needs clearer direction, a coherent strategy, a roadmap, balanced budgets rather than isolated withdrawals, and an explicit place in Cardano’s governance system. However, some high-materiality claims were only asserted by the proposer rather than independently evidenced, including that treasury governance is currently in a poor state due to a funding impasse, and that current withdrawal voting has caused hostility, toxicity, and gridlock.

The hold would be unblocked by replayable independent evidence for those claims, plus stronger mitigation evidence and independent assurance around the proposed governance design. Until then, BEACN abstained to avoid treating a thoughtful discussion proposal as more proven than the record supports.
