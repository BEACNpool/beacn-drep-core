# Scalus 2026: Maintenance, Dijkstra Readiness, Interoperability & Application Runtime
**BEACN DRep vote: NO**  _(plain-language explanation — precomputed)_

BEACN voted NO because this was a large treasury withdrawal with seven unresolved review blockers, including missing budget, feasibility, risk, alternatives, failure-mode, and community impact analysis. The proposal may describe useful public infrastructure, but the evidence supplied did not meet the higher bar required for spending shared ADA.

The action requested ₳2,464,844 for a 9-month continuation of Scalus by Lantr Engineering, through March 2027. The scope included ongoing maintenance, Dijkstra readiness, improved JS/TS interfaces and documentation, Java/Kotlin interoperability, and foundational application runtime components. The proposal also said this was a reduced resubmission from a prior ₳8.5M, 12-month request, with the L1 node, full L2 integration, and broad formal verification removed.

Some claims were supported in the proposal, especially the amount requested, the 9-month duration, the stated dollar equivalent at the proposal’s reference rate, and the reduced scope compared with the prior version. But several important claims were still only asserted or thinly evidenced, including Scalus’s established delivery history, reuse across named Cardano developer tools, direct use by named projects, execution controls, and risk mitigations. The review specifically found missing independent evidence for key adoption and platform-history claims.

The anchor document was available, so the action could be reviewed, and the counterargument pass found a reasonable YES case around continued funding for open-source Cardano infrastructure. However, the treasury analysis was incomplete, the risk review was thin, the synthesis was blocked, and treasury conditions carried additional caution. BEACN’s NO is therefore an evidence-based rejection of this funding request as presented, not a rejection of Scalus as a project.
