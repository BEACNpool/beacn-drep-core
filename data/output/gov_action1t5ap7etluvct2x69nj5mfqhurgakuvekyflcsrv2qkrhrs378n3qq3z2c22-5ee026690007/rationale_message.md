# Reforming Treasury Governance
**BEACN DRep vote: ABSTAIN**  _(plain-language explanation — precomputed)_

BEACN voted ABSTAIN because the proposal raises a legitimate governance discussion, but its threshold for a directional vote was not met: several central claims about treasury dysfunction, hostility, and gridlock were asserted without enough independent, replayable evidence.

The action was an InfoAction asking the ecosystem to discuss reforms to Cardano treasury governance, including strategic budgeting, dedicated entities, expert commissions, budget guardrails, and a sequence of future votes around the NCL, budget, and treasury withdrawal. The proposal’s strongest supported case is that treasury governance should provide clearer direction, a coherent strategy, and a roadmap, and that DReps should consider balanced budgets rather than isolated project withdrawals competing against each other.

However, the review found the evidence base thin. The proposal’s broader diagnosis, including claims that the current system is in a poor state, stuck in a funding impasse, and producing hostility or ecosystem-harming conflict, was not independently supported in the supplied record. The anchor document was available and replayable, execution risk was assessed as low, and the strongest YES case cleared the evidence gates, but confidence was reduced by a flag score and by missing mitigation evidence and independent assurance.

This abstention is a conservative, evidence-based hold, not active opposition. A directional vote would be unblocked by stronger independent evidence for the claimed treasury governance dysfunction, conflict, and funding impasse, along with clearer mitigation or assurance evidence for the proposed reform path.
