# Reforming Treasury Governance
**BEACN DRep vote: ABSTAIN**  _(plain-language explanation — precomputed)_

BEACN voted ABSTAIN because the review did not have enough distribution and evidence support to make a directional vote. This is a conservative, evidence-based hold, not a rejection of the topic.

The action asked to open public discussion on reforming Cardano treasury governance and to outline guardrails for strategic direction. The proposal argued that treasury governance is in poor shape because of a funding impasse, scarce-resource competition, hostility, toxic conflict, and gridlock. Those diagnoses were treated as proposer assertions rather than independently supported facts in the review.

Several parts were supported inside the proposal itself: a dedicated investment budget with strategic direction, a roadmap, thematic structure, allocation percentages, balanced-budget voting by DReps, a vote-appointed strategic entity, and a vote-appointed expert commission. The pinned anchor was available and replayable, and the strongest YES case cleared the evidence gates for the proposed budget structure. But the broader evidence base was still thin, execution risk was unknown, mitigation evidence and independent assurance were missing, and no DRep distribution was available.

A directional vote would need the missing DRep distribution, independent evidence for the core claims about dysfunction and gridlock, and clearer risk mitigation or assurance for the proposed governance changes. Until then, abstaining avoids overstating confidence while recognizing that the proposal raises a legitimate area for discussion.
