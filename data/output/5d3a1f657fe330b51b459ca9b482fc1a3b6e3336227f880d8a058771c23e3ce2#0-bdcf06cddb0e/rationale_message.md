# Reforming Treasury Governance
**BEACN DRep vote: ABSTAIN**  _(plain-language explanation — precomputed)_

BEACN voted ABSTAIN because the proposal raises serious and relevant treasury-governance ideas, but the evidence did not meet the threshold for a directional vote. This is a conservative, evidence-based hold, not active opposition.

The action asked the governance community, DReps, and ecosystem participants to discuss reforms to Cardano treasury governance, including strategic budgeting, dedicated entities, expert evaluation, a voting sequence, and an eight-part thematic budget structure. The proposal was reviewable because its anchor document was pinned and replayable, and several claims were well-supported inside the proposal itself: that treasury governance needs clearer direction, coherent strategy, a roadmap, constitutional or governance-system support, and a budget-centered process rather than isolated project withdrawals.

The main weakness was evidence quality. Two high-materiality problem statements were asserted without independent support: that Cardano treasury governance is in a poor state because of a funding impasse and insufficient direct support, and that current withdrawal voting has caused competition, hostility, toxicity, and ecosystem-harming conflict. The risk review was also thin, with execution risk unknown and missing mitigation evidence and independent assurance. A flag score was present, which reduced confidence.

A directional YES would need replayable independent evidence for the major problem claims, plus clearer mitigation and assurance evidence for the governance and execution risks. Until then, abstaining avoids overstating certainty while still recognizing that the proposal makes a defensible case for clearer treasury strategy and budget process reform.
