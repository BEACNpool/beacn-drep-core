BEACN voted ABSTAIN because a required baseline field for a treasury withdrawal was missing: `treasury_amount_lovelace`. Without that, the review could not responsibly complete a directional vote, even though the proposal text stated a 4,094,000 ADA request.

It also named Mill Law Firm for quarterly independent financial reviews.

The treasury analysis also lacked milestone-gated disbursement and sustainability-path evidence, and the risk review still lacked independent assurance and a dependency map.

This ABSTAIN is a conservative, evidence-based hold, not a rejection of the program idea. A directional vote would require the missing treasury withdrawal baseline field, especially `treasury_amount_lovelace`, plus stronger evidence for the shutdown/replacement mechanism, milestone-gated disbursement, sustainability path, independent assurance, and dependency mapping.
