# Net Change Limit: Cardano Treasury (Epochs 613-713)
**BEACN DRep vote: ABSTAIN**  _(plain-language explanation — precomputed)_

BEACN voted ABSTAIN because the review did not reach the evidence threshold for a directional vote. The proposal’s core request was clear and supported: it asks DReps to agree to a 500,000,000 ada Net Change Limit for Cardano Treasury withdrawals from the start of Epoch 613 on February 13, 2026 through the close of Epoch 713, on or about July 3, 2027.

The proposal says this cap would replace any prior Net Change Limit for the same period, count Treasury withdrawals already debited during the period toward the total, and would not itself authorize any direct Treasury withdrawal. The review found those central mechanics well-supported in the proposal anchor, including that the action is an InfoAction with no direct on-chain withdrawal effect.

The hold came from thinner evidence around important supporting claims. The proposal asserted that Yes votes above 50% of active voting stake are sufficient under TREASURY-01a, and that the existing 350,000,000 ada limit has been useful but is becoming constrained through mid-2027, but the review did not find independent evidence for those claims. Risk review was also thin, with execution risk unknown and missing mitigation or independent assurance evidence.

This ABSTAIN is a conservative, evidence-based hold, not active opposition. A directional vote would be unblocked by replayable independent evidence for the claimed DRep ratification threshold, evidence that the current 350,000,000 ada limit is becoming constrained, and clearer mitigation or assurance evidence for the risk review.
