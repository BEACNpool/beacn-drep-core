# Reforming Treasury Governance
**BEACN DRep vote: ABSTAIN**  _(plain-language explanation — precomputed)_

BEACN voted ABSTAIN because the proposal raises a relevant treasury-governance issue, but the evidence did not meet the threshold for a directional vote. This is a conservative, evidence-based hold, not active opposition.

The action was an InfoAction asking to introduce ideas for reforming Cardano treasury governance into public discussion. It outlined guardrails for strategic direction, suggested a sequence of votes on the NCL, budget, and overall treasury withdrawal, and proposed a thematic budget structure with eight domains. The proposer argued that treasury governance is in a poor state, that current withdrawal voting has contributed to conflict and perceived competition, and that Cardano needs clearer treasury direction, strategy, and roadmap.

Some parts were supported well enough for review: the proposal clearly argued that treasury governance should be addressed by Cardano’s governance system and constitution, recommended a dedicated investment budget, proposed thematic budgeting, and described a possible strategic entity and expert commission. But several high-materiality claims remained unsupported by independent evidence, especially the claims about a current funding impasse, ecosystem-harming toxicity, and the limits of DRep expertise. The review also noted thin evidence overall, missing mitigation evidence, missing independent assurance, and a confidence-reducing flag score.

The anchor document was available and replayable, execution risk was assessed as low, and the strongest YES case passed: the proposal substantiated that treasury governance deserves explicit constitutional and governance-system attention. Still, because key claims could not be tied to stronger public evidence, BEACN abstained to avoid overstating certainty. A directional vote would be unblocked by independent evidence for the claimed treasury-governance dysfunction and conflict, plus clearer mitigation and assurance evidence for the proposed governance approach.
